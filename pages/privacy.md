# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 2025_

Moxie Color LLC (“MoxieColor,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information in connection with our website, services, and products.

---

## Who We Are
Moxie Color LLC is a Washington State limited liability company, owned and operated by Jennifer Chin.  
**Contact:** [jchin@moxiecolor.com](mailto:jchin@moxiecolor.com)  
We act as the privacy contact for this site.

---

## Information We Collect
We do not directly collect personal information through this website. Instead, we rely on trusted third-party services to provide our newsletter, storefront, and affiliate offerings:

- **Newsletter** (Mailchimp): If you sign up, we collect your email and (optionally) first name.  
- **Digital downloads & checkout** (Gumroad): When you purchase or download, Gumroad collects and processes your information.  
- **Affiliate links** (Amazon): If you click an affiliate link, Amazon may collect browsing and purchase information.  
- **Printed products** (Spoonflower): Orders and customer information are handled by Spoonflower.

If you email us directly, we will use your email address only to respond and delete it once the matter is resolved.

---

## Cookies & Tracking
We do not set cookies or tracking pixels ourselves. However, third parties (such as Gumroad, Amazon, or Mailchimp) may use cookies or similar technologies when you interact with their services. See their privacy policies for details.  

We may use URL parameters (such as UTM tags) to understand which pages, buttons, or links on our site lead to sign-ups or purchases. These parameters only record general referral information (e.g., which button was clicked) and do not collect personally identifiable information.

---

## How We Use Information
- To deliver requested products, downloads, or newsletters  
- To respond to inquiries you send us directly  
- To analyze general site traffic using built-in hosting logs (no personal identifiers stored)
- When you click a purchase button on our site, Gumroad’s secure overlay checkout may open directly on the page. All transaction details, including payment processing, are handled by Gumroad according to their privacy policy. We do not see or store your payment details.
- Affiliate and partner links may help support the site; we may receive small commissions if you purchase through them. These links are clearly disclosed on our pages.

---

## Data Retention
- **Emails:** deleted after resolution of your inquiry.  
- **Newsletter (Mailchimp):** retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.  
- **Third-party providers:** data is retained according to their own policies (see links below).

## Your Rights

- Request correction or deletion of your personal data  
- Opt out of certain uses or sales of your information  
- Withdraw consent (e.g., unsubscribe from our newsletter)

To exercise these rights, contact us at [jchin@moxiecolor.com](mailto:jchin@moxiecolor.com). We will work with our providers, as applicable, to honor your request.
