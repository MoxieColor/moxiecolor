# You Are Your Own Sysadmin
**Completed Sept 2025**  

**You Are Your Own Sysadmin** is a neon manifesto for autonomy in an age of surveillance. This series twists geek humor and underground art into unapologetic posters that laugh, snarl, and glow where conformity expects silence. 

{% include assets-grid.html captions=true layout="masonry" %}

We are told we have no choice. Sit still, play nice, consume what we’re fed, accept the leash. But that’s lazy sysadmin-ing of the self.

The **You Are Your Own Sysadmin** series is my refusal to obey that script. These neon posters are part manifesto, part inside joke — a reminder that autonomy doesn’t have to be tidy or respectable. Sometimes the best choice sucks, sometimes it takes work, but it’s still yours to make.

Each piece — Neon Kiss, Neon Bow Tie, Neon Good Pets, and Chaotic Pets — is an art intervention against surveillance culture and spoon-fed consumerism. They laugh, jeer, growl, and howl in six unruly colorways, glowing like protest signs in spaces that beg you to comply.

High-resolution rebellion - root access for your walls
