<h2>How to Print &amp; Fold</h2>

<div class="grid">
  <div class="card">
    <img src="{{ '/assets/images/stores/moxie-zine-v1-cover.webp' | relative_url }}"
         alt="Zine print layout preview — double-sided single sheet.">
    <h3>1) Print double-sided</h3>
    <p class="muted">Use US Letter (8.5×11). Turn off “fit to page.” Print at 100% for clean folds.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="card">
    <img src="{{ '/assets/images/how-to-fold-a-zine.png' | relative_url }}"
         alt="Zine fold diagram — adapted from Wikibooks zine making guide.">
    <h3>2) Fold &amp; slice</h3>
    <p class="muted">Fold in half, then into quarters, slit the center seam, refold into an 8-page booklet.</p>
    <p class="muted">
      <a href="https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Zine_Making/Putting_pages_together" target="_blank" rel="noopener">
        Folding instructions (Wikibooks) →
      </a>
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="card">
    <img src="{{ '/assets/images/stores/moxie-zine-v1-mini-poster.webp' | relative_url }}"
         alt="Mini poster inside the zine reading Never Forget — You are your own sysadmin.">
    <h3>3) Mini poster inside</h3>
    <p class="muted">Open to the center — that’s your mini poster. Tape, frame, or paste responsibly.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<h2>What this is about</h2>
<p>“Never Forget — You are your own sysadmin.” It’s a reminder to set your own permissions in art, work, and life.</p>
